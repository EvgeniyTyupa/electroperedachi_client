Loyalty checkout update

Guests and zero-balance customers see the normal purchase form, with no login link or popup. Signed-in customers can apply their maximum eligible points in one click, or remove them. Server quotes determine the payable amount shown on the submit button in all five supported event forms. Email and phone fields and cart selections are retained. Point-service errors allow purchase without points.

Verified on isolated fixtures in the actual Vice City form: apply/remove, quantity changes, 30% cap, guest and zero-balance paths, and quote failure fallback. No real payments or emails were sent.
